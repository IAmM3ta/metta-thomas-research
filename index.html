ABOUT

A bit about me

I’m a multidisciplinary engineer and builder working at the intersection of spatial interfaces, systems thinking, and emerging technologies. My background spans engineering physics, data-driven systems design, and large-scale project delivery across Europe, North America, Asia, and Oceania.

After working in a high-tech incubator within TU Delft’s aerospace and materials science engineering laboratory — in partnership with the European Space Agency and NOVU — I’ve spent the last several years independently developing tools and platforms that blend rigorous technical foundations with intuitive, human-centered design. My current focus is on spatial computing, WebXR experiences, and production-grade interfaces for complex systems — particularly within the emerging personal electric vehicle and infrastructure space.

I’m drawn to problems that sit at the edge of what’s technically possible and what’s actually useful. I value clear thinking, collaborative problem-solving, and building things that hold up under real-world conditions. When I’m not working, you’ll usually find me traveling, making music, or exploring new ways of seeing and representing space.
